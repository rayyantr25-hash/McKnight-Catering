Scratch-made, course by course — from breakfast tacos to plated dinners.

I. Scratch-Made

Menus built by the chef, not a template.

Every McKnight's menu is chef-curated — scratch cooking rooted in real technique, seasonal ingredients, and a hospitality sensibility that runs through breakfast trays and formal plated dinners alike.

Menus are organized by course and easily customized for dietary needs, budget, and the tone of your event.
